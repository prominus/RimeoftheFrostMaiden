## Wondrous item, common

This hooded lantern burns for **12 hours** on **1 pint of magical oil**, shedding bright light in a **30-foot-radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet**. While the lantern is within **300 feet** of any creature it is attuned to, its flame turns **bright green**. The lantern doesn't pinpoint the creature's exact location.

Specific magical oils, enhanced with divination magic, are used to power this object. Once an oil is used in the lantern, it must be either disposed of or burnt before another can be put in.

The lantern can act as a normal hooded lantern using regular oil, shedding bright light in a 30-foot-radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. Each pint of normal oil lasts 6 hours.
