Null was the dragon god of death, undeath, time and fate, worshipped under two aspects, Chronepsis and Falazure.

**As Chronepsis**, known as _the Guardian of the Lost_, he traveled through time to rectify past wrongs, measured the lives of dragons across the multiverse with his hourglasses, and ferried the souls of dead dragons to their respective planes, granting them eternal sanctuary from the foes of their former lives.

**As Falazure**, known as _the Reaver_ and the _Night Dragon_, he was revered by evil dragons and reveled in the taking of life, bestowing blessings upon those who aided his grim work. He was regarded as the creator of the first undead dragons, such as dracoliches and vampire dragons.
